# Architectural Decisions and Round-2 Challenges

> Status: **Decisions final** (from the review on 2026-09-30). The challenges below were found while applying them, and their resolutions are reflected in every other document.

## 1. Final MVP decisions

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Tax | Configurable master (`taxes`). Prices are **tax-exclusive by default**. Tax-on-service is configurable per tax. Tax-inclusive prices are supported. |
| Service charge | Configurable master (`service_charges`), separate from tax, with a configurable rate. |
| Tax assignment | Taxes and service charges attach to **charge codes** through mapping tables. A rate plan chooses a room charge code and a price mode. See R3. |
| Calculation | One pure, country-agnostic **Charge Calculation Engine** (`internal/chargecalc`) is used by every module. |
| Check-in | `CLEAN` is enough. `properties.require_room_inspection_for_checkin` (default `false`) makes `INSPECTED` mandatory. |
| Night audit | **Blocked** by unresolved arrivals. Staff resolve them explicitly, and there's a bulk "Mark remaining as no-show" action. Nothing is ever auto-no-showed. |
| Guest | Tenant-wide profile. Reservations, stays, folios, payments and rooms stay property-scoped. What a user can see is controlled by permissions. |
| User | Email is unique **per tenant**. A user belongs to one tenant and has access to many properties through `user_properties`. |
| Currency | One currency per property (`properties.currency_code`). No multi-currency transactions. |
| Business date | Required and property-specific. It's managed by `business_days`, is distinct from server time, and only moves forward through night audit. |
| Occupancy | Derived from reservations and stays. There's no `rooms.status`. |
| Housekeeping | Separate from occupancy: `CLEAN`, `DIRTY`, `CLEANING` or `INSPECTED`. |
| Room block | A separate `room_blocks` concept for OOO and OOS. |

## 2. Round-2 challenges: remaining problems and contradictions

| # | Problem | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | The suggested `taxes.is_inclusive` **contradicts** `rate_plans.is_tax_inclusive`. Two places decide the same thing, and they can disagree: a room could be inclusive per the rate plan while its VAT row says exclusive. | **Inclusivity is a property of the *price*, not of the tax.** The same 11% VAT applies whether the price was quoted gross or net. `taxes` has no inclusive flag. The price mode lives on `rate_plans` (room revenue) and on `charge_codes` (the default for manual postings), and it is snapshotted on each nightly rate and each folio item. |
| **R2** | "Tax-inclusive" is ambiguous when a service charge also exists. Does 1,221,000 "inclusive" include the service charge too? | **`INCLUSIVE` means the price includes every service charge and tax mapped to the charge code** (the common "nett" price). It's stored as `price_mode varchar` (`EXCLUSIVE`/`INCLUSIVE`) rather than a boolean, so a future `TAX_INCLUSIVE_SERVICE_EXCLUSIVE` mode is a new CHECK value, not a schema migration. |
| **R3** | Option A (`rate_plans.tax_id` + `service_charge_id`) can't hold multiple taxes. Option B as a mapping *on the rate plan* doesn't cover breakfast, laundry or minibar, which have no rate plan. | **Option C: tax and service rules attach to charge codes** through `charge_code_taxes` and `charge_code_service_charges` (many-to-many). A rate plan only points to its `room_charge_code_id`. There is one resolution path for every charge source: *charge code → rules*. A rate plan that needs different treatment (for example a tax-exempt government rate) points to a different charge code, such as `ROOM_EXEMPT` with no taxes mapped. |
| **R4** | A single `folio_items.tax_amount` can't hold two taxes, and it loses *which* rate was applied. Tax filing needs a breakdown per tax. | Add **`folio_item_components`**: one row per applied tax or service charge, with a snapshot of its code, rate, taxable base and amount. `folio_items` keeps the totals, and a deferred constraint trigger checks that they match the component sums. |
| **R5** | Editing a tax rate in place (for example VAT going from 11% to 12%) must not rewrite history. | Posted items **snapshot** the rate and amounts, so history is always correct. An edit affects only future postings. Rates are looked up in exactly one place (`billing.ChargeRuleResolver`), so adding effective-dated rate periods later is an additive change. |
| **R6** | Extracting tax from an inclusive price produces rounding residue. For example, 1,000,000 inclusive of 11% gives 900,900.90 net + 99,099.10 tax. | The engine computes the components from the extracted net and then **adds the residual to net**, so the total always equals the quoted gross exactly. The guest never sees 1,000,000.01. |
| **R7** | Rounding precision isn't only a currency question. ISO 4217 says IDR has 2 decimals, but hotels bill whole rupiah. | Add `properties.currency_decimals` (0–3), set at property setup. The engine rounds half away from zero at that precision. Rounding mode is an engine parameter and isn't hard-coded to any country. |
| **R8** | A `properties.business_date` column **plus** a day-management table would be two sources of truth. | **`business_days` is the only source.** Exactly one row per property has `status = OPEN` (enforced by a partial unique index), and that row is the current business date. The column on `properties` is removed. |
| **R9** | Terminology clash: the night-audit example calls not-yet-arrived bookings "PENDING". In the Phase-1 model, `PENDING` was the *draft* line status, which does not hold inventory. | Rename the draft line status to **`DRAFT`**. An **unresolved arrival** is precisely `reservation_rooms.status = 'CONFIRMED' AND arrival_date <= business_date`. Draft lines never block night audit and only show as a warning. |
| **R10** | Only unresolved *arrivals* were specified. Unresolved **departures** (in-house guests with `departure_date <= business_date`) have the same problem: night audit would post a room charge to a guest who may have left, or leave an unexplained overstay. | Night audit is **also blocked by unresolved departures**. Staff either check the guest out or extend the stay. There is no bulk action for departures, because every checkout needs a settled folio. |
| **R11** | With email unique per tenant, `admin@hotel.com` can exist in two tenants, so **login needs a tenant identifier**. | `POST /auth/login` takes `tenant_code` + `email` + `password`. The tenant can later be resolved from a subdomain without changing the schema. |
| **R12** | Tenant-wide guests versus property-scoped access: can a front-desk user in Bali see a Jakarta guest's history? | Profile and history are separated. See Business Rules §20: tenant-wide profile search needs `guest.search_all`, and stay history outside the user's own properties needs `guest.history_all_properties`. `guests.origin_property_id` records where a profile was created. Tenant admins bypass these checks. |
| **R13** | `payments.currency_code` is redundant with one currency per property, and it can't be CHECKed against the property. | Remove it. A property's currency and decimals become **immutable** once it has any financial transaction (enforced by the service and a trigger). Multi-currency later adds `transaction_currency` and `exchange_rate` columns; that's additive. |
| **R14** | Multiple roles per user per property versus one. | **One role per (user, property)**: `user_properties(user_id, property_id, role_id)`. `users.is_tenant_admin` grants everything in the tenant. Moving to multiple roles later is a mechanical migration. |
| **R15** | The suggested engine input includes "charge type". If the engine knows about ROOM, LAUNDRY and so on, business rules leak into math. | **The engine is pure math.** Its inputs are amounts, a price mode, and lists of service-charge and tax rules. The resolver turns a charge code into rules. The engine never reads the database and never knows the country. |
| **R16** | Future POS systems usually calculate their own tax. Recalculating in the PMS could disagree by rounding. | The engine has a **`Verify`** mode: an integration posts its own breakdown, and the PMS validates it within a tolerance and stores it as given (`source = INTEGRATION`). This is designed now and built later. |
| **R17** | The order of discount, service and tax is undefined. | Discount applies **first**. On an exclusive price it's taken off net. On an inclusive price it's taken off gross, and then the components are extracted. Service charge is calculated on net. Tax is calculated on net, plus service when `tax_on_service`. |
| **R18** | Multiple taxes: is tax charged on tax (compound)? | **No compounding in the MVP.** Each tax is computed independently on its own base. `taxes.calculation_order` and `includes_prior_taxes` can be added later as additive columns. |
| **R19** | An "Invoice" was mentioned as an engine consumer. | Invoices are **not an MVP entity**. The folio is the guest statement. A future fiscal invoice will reference folio items and use its own gapless numbering, which is why folio numbers must not double as invoice numbers. |
| **R20** | `numeric(14,2)` caps at about 1 trillion. That's fine per item in IDR, but tight for group folios and report sums in some currencies. | Use `numeric(18,2)` for all money. |
| **R21** | Tax exemption for a guest or company (diplomats, government). | This is future work. The engine input has `ExemptTaxIDs` from day one. In the MVP the exemption is achieved with an exempt charge code (R3). |

## 3. Round-1 challenges (still adopted, summary)

C1 Dates and occupancy live only on reservation lines · C2 each line has its own lifecycle · C3 nightly rate snapshots · C4 one stay per reservation line, and stay rooms are sequential segments · C5 walk-in = reservation · C6 folio belongs to the reservation and is linked to a stay at check-in · C7 the ledger is the only balance · C8 a refund is a new payment · C9 charge codes and immutable signed items · C10 business date · C11 half-open ranges · C12 blocks are ACTIVE or CANCELLED only · C13 composite FKs · C14 layered double-booking protection · C15 effective room type for upgrades · C16 module-first Go layout · C17 no `reservation_date` · C18 idempotency keys · C19 gapless document sequences · C20 housekeeping kept off the `rooms` lock row.
